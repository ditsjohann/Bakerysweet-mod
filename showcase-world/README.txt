Sweetheart Bakery - Showcase World (Minecraft 1.21.1, Fabric + Fabric API + Sweetheart Bakery)

Unzip into your .minecraft/saves folder, then open "Sweetheart Bakery Showcase" (creative mode).

You spawn on the plaza:
  south  - the Sweetheart Bakery shop: furnished, café cats, coffee corner, garden front and terrace
  east   - the Kitchen Hall: every machine in action, a coffee bar, placed treats, floors & wallpapers,
           all the decorations in rows, and a little café where customers come in
  west   - the Farm: every crop fully grown, and all seven fruit trees
  north  - the Wild Meadow with a lily pond, then the Woods street (a little house for each wood)

It is always a sunny noon (no day cycle, no weather, no mobs) so screenshots and videos look the same every time.
Press F1 to hide the hand and hotbar. /time set and /weather work if you want a sunset or rain.
